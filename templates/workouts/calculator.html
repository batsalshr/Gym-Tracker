About 1RM

One Rep Max (1RM) is the maximum weight you can lift for a single repetition with proper form.

Formulas Used:

  • Epley: weight × (1 + reps/30)
  • Brzycki: weight × 36 / (37 - reps)
  • Lander: 100 × weight / (101.3 - 2.67 × reps)

Tips:

  • Most accurate with 1-10 reps
  • Use a weight you can lift with good form
  • Results are estimates, actual 1RM may vary
