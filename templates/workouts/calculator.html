About 1RM

One Rep Max (1RM) is the maximum weight you can lift for a single repetition with proper form.

Formulas Used:

  • Epley: weight x (1 + reps/30)
  • Brzycki: weight x 36 / (37 - reps)
  • Lander: 100 x weight / (101.3 - 2.67 x reps)

Tips:

  • Most accurate with 1-10 reps
  • Use a weight you can lift with good form
  • Results are estimates, actual 1RM may vary

About BMI

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a measure of body fat based on height and weight.

BMI Categories:

  • Underweight: Below 18.5
  • Normal: 18.5 - 24.9
  • Overweight: 25.0 - 29.9
  • Obese: 30.0 and above

Limitations:

  • Does not account for muscle mass
  • Athletes may have higher BMI due to muscle
  • Use alongside other health metrics
